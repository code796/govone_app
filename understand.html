Understand GovOne

  1. Skip or watch the GovOne introduction.
  2. Log in with your account or register a new account.
  3. Use the dashboard cards for applications, complaints, schemes, immigration, cyber guidelines, banking, news and bills.
  4. Use Files to review applications, payments, dates and statuses.
  5. Use Search, Language, Accessibility, Settings and Account controls in the top bar.
  6. Use More Queries, FAQs and Feedback whenever assistance is needed.
