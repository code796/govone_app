About GovOne

GovOne is a citizen-focused public-service interface that brings applications, complaints, bills, schemes, trusted notices and official portals together in one clear place. The platform is designed to make public services easier to discover, understand and access through a simple, responsive and accessible experience.

Core features

  • Applications, registration and status tracking
  • Bill and payment links
  • Government schemes and detailed notifications
  • Immigration, cyber-guideline, banking and news sections
  • Accessibility, language and account controls

Important: Verify information and complete official transactions only on the linked government or service-provider website.
